Entry Level Buyer Success Story

March 9, 2025 · Success Story · 1 min read

We're thrilled to share an inspiring story about how Baxter & Mason Property Buyers made a real difference in the life of a local single mum!

With a budget under $500,000 and taking advantage of the 2% deposit government scheme designed for single parents, she was determined to break into the property market.

Juggling her busy life, she decided to enlist the help of a buyers agent to navigate the often overwhelming process of buying a home, especially since dealing with real estate selling agents wasn't her favorite pastime.

After some diligent searching, we were able to secure a fantastic 2-bedroom unit for her, which came with a spacious bonus room. This extra space is a game-changer, as she plans to transform it into another bedroom and a home office, allowing her to comfortably accommodate her two teenage kids without the hefty price tag of a three-bedroom apartment. It's amazing how a little creativity can turn a modest property into a perfect family home!

The path to closing this deal was anything but smooth, filled with challenges along the way. However, through teamwork and persistence, we collaborated closely with the selling agent Nicky Ellem at Keyline Realty to ensure everything fell into place.

Seeing the joy on our buyer's face when we handed her the keys was absolutely priceless. We're so proud to have played a part in helping her achieve her dream of homeownership!

Sally Blyth

Sally is the founder and director of Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent. She bought her first property in 1996 and has been helping buyers, especially women building independence on their own, ever since.
