Success Story

Josh's Off-Market Win

November 26, 2025 · Success Story · 1 min read

For many buyers, the property search can feel like an endless cycle of hope and disappointment. Josh knew this feeling all too well - after months of searching, he continually missed out on properties in the competitive Sunshine Coast market.

That's when he decided to partner with Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent.

Within just three days of signing up, Sally identified and secured an off-market property perfectly suited to Josh's needs. The deal didn't come easy - it required late-night negotiations on a Friday to ensure the property was secured before it slipped away. But that's the kind of dedication she brings to every client: persistence, clear communication, and a commitment to getting the best outcome.

Why Josh's Story Stands Out

Speed & Strategy: After months of missed opportunities, Josh had a property secured in just three days.

Off-Market Advantage: Leveraging agent connections meant Josh avoided the stress of competing in a multiple-offer situation.

Dedication: Negotiating late into the night ensured the deal was locked in quickly and smoothly.

Professional Support: Clear communication and market knowledge gave Josh confidence throughout the process.

Josh's journey is a reminder that with the right guidance, persistence, and local expertise, even the most challenging property searches can turn into success stories.

Sally Blyth

Sally is the founder and director of Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent. She bought her first property in 1996 and has been helping buyers, especially women building independence on their own, ever since.
