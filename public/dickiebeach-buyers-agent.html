Dickie Beach Buyers Agent.

Older beach houses beside the surf club, bought with the renovation scope priced in.

Dickie Beach, officially spelled Dicky Beach and named after the steamship SS Dicky that ran aground on this shore long ago, is a surf club, holiday park and beach-shack suburb on the Caloundra side of Currimundi.

What it is like to buy in Dickie Beach

The housing leans old: fibro and brick beach houses, small units and the occasional new build close to the sand. Buyers include first-home buyers who want a beach address, families who see a house they can improve over time, and investors who plan to renovate or redevelop in the long run.

The suburb sits between Moffat Beach to the south and Currimundi to the north, with Caloundra just beyond, and most buyers look at all of them together before deciding where the money goes furthest.

Who buys in Dickie Beach

First-home buyers often look here because a beach address does not have to mean an apartment. Others buy a house they can live in now and improve over time, or hold a site for the longer term. Each approach suits different properties, so we begin by working out which one you are, then match the building, the block and the street to that plan rather than chasing whatever looks best in photos.

Trade-offs to weigh before you buy

Dickie Beach rewards patient buyers who look past the cosmetics.

  • Old stock and renovation scope: tin roofs, timber stumps, tired wiring and termite history can add up quickly.
  • Redevelopment hopes: zoning and overlays decide what is possible, not agent talk or a neighbour’s rumour.
  • Coastal hazard: dune-front and low properties may be affected by erosion and storm tide mapping.
  • Summer foot traffic and parking around the surf club and holiday park precinct.
  • Older units: levies, sinking funds and building condition vary a great deal between blocks.

How we help Dickie Beach buyers

Our work here is mostly about turning old stock into a clear price. We inspect in person, read the building and pest report, and tell you what a tired house will cost to put right so that the number you offer reflects it. If a block looks like it might offer redevelopment potential, we check the zoning and overlays before you pay for the possibility.

Because many buyers compare Dickie Beach with Moffat Beach, Currimundi and Caloundra, we do the same, using recent sales to show where your budget gets the best land, condition and walk to the beach.

Buyers we represent in Dickie Beach receive the same in-person inspections, overlay checks and negotiation support we use across the entire Sunshine Coast.
