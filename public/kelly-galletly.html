Kelly Galletly.

Buyers agent at Baxter & Mason, helping Sunshine Coast buyers buy with clarity and care.

Buyers agent

With more than 20 years of experience in sales and customer service, Kelly brings a strong foundation in communication, negotiation and relationship-building to the Sunshine Coast real estate market. She is known for being honest, hardworking and genuinely committed to helping people achieve their property goals with clarity, confidence and care.

Tel: 0405 079 989

Lived experience in property

Having personally bought, sold and managed multiple investment properties, Kelly understands the excitement, pressure and emotion that come with real estate decisions. Her focus is on making the buying process smooth, stress-free and well-supported, turning what can feel overwhelming into a positive and rewarding experience.

At home on the Sunshine Coast

For the past 18 years, Kelly has proudly called the Sunshine Coast home. She is raising her two boys here while running two successful local businesses, and her passion for property is driven by a genuine desire to help families, first-home buyers and investors find their place in this incredible community. Whether it is beachside, hinterland or anywhere from Caloundra to Noosa, she is dedicated to guiding clients through every step with professionalism and heart.
