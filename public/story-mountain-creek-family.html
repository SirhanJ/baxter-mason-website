A Fresh Start With Room to Grow: A Single Mum's Family Home in Mountain Creek

September 25, 2026 · Success Story · 4 min read

In short: Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent helped a single mum of three buy a four bedroom family home in Mountain Creek on the Sunshine Coast. The home has a pool, two living areas and solar. It is close to high school and sits in a suburb with good long-term growth potential. Sally Blyth handled the search, due diligence and negotiation from start to settlement.

Starting again is a big step, and doing it with three children depending on you makes it even bigger. When this wonderful single mum came to Baxter & Mason, she was ready for a fresh start. She wanted a home that gave her family stability, security and a place that was truly theirs.

The brief: enough bedrooms for a family of four

Her first priority was simple: a bedroom for everyone. But Sally could see this purchase needed to do more than solve today's problem.

Children grow quickly. A home that suits primary schoolers can feel cramped once teenagers need their own space, somewhere to study and room to breathe. So the goal became a home the family could grow into, not out of.

What we found in Mountain Creek

Sally searched with longevity in mind and secured a Mountain Creek home that delivered on every front:

  • Four large bedrooms, giving each child their own space now and through the teenage years
  • A swimming pool for summer weekends at home (read our guide to buying a house with a pool)
  • Two separate living areas, so the family has room to spread out
  • Solar power to help reduce electricity costs
  • Close to high school, for easy school runs for years to come
  • A solid, well-built home ready to move into and make their own

Why Mountain Creek was the right suburb

Mountain Creek is an established family suburb in the heart of the Sunshine Coast, between Buderim, Sippy Downs and Mooloolaba. For this family it offered three things:

  • Schools. Mountain Creek State High School is in the suburb, along with other school options nearby.
  • Lifestyle. Parks, walking tracks and easy access to the beaches at Mooloolaba.
  • Long-term value. Mountain Creek has good growth potential, so the home supports future capital growth as well as family life today.

For a single parent, the right suburb matters as much as the right house. It shapes school runs, support networks and how the home builds wealth over time.

How Sally helped from search to settlement

Sally managed every step of the purchase, including:

  • Building a clear brief around the family's needs now and in ten years
  • Searching on and off market across suitable suburbs
  • Inspecting properties and arranging building and pest checks
  • Reviewing the contract and completing due diligence
  • Negotiating the price and terms on her behalf
  • Guiding and supporting her through to settlement

That meant this busy mum could focus on her children, knowing someone was protecting her interests at every stage. It is the same support we offer every woman buying property on her own.

A new place to call home

Settlement day brought keys, smiles and the start of a new chapter. For this family it wasn't just a house. It was a fresh start, with room to grow.

"It was a pleasure to buy this Mountain Creek home and give them a new place to call home."

Sally Blyth, Director and Buyers Advocate, Baxter & Mason

Frequently asked questions

Can a single mum buy a house on her own on the Sunshine Coast?

Yes. Many single mums buy on the Sunshine Coast on one income. The key is a clear budget, a mortgage broker's pre-approval and a brief focused on what the family needs long term. A buyers agent can guide you through the process and negotiate for you.

How many bedrooms does a family of four need?

Most families with three children look for four bedrooms, so each child has their own room. Planning for the teenage years helps you avoid having to sell and buy again a few years later.

Is Mountain Creek a good suburb for families?

Mountain Creek is popular with families because of its schools, parks and central location between Buderim, Sippy Downs and Mooloolaba. It is also an established suburb with good long-term growth potential.

What does a buyers agent do for a single parent?

A buyers agent searches for suitable homes, inspects them, completes due diligence and negotiates the price, and supports you through to settlement. For single parents, it saves time and reduces the stress of buying alone.

How much does Baxter & Mason charge?

Baxter & Mason charges one clear, full service fee rather than a percentage of the purchase price. Book a free discovery call for details.

Your next chapter
