Sunshine Coast

Noosa Buyers Agent.

Noosa's own rules, holiday-letting limits and waterfront risks, explained.

Noosa covers Noosa Heads, Noosa Junction, Noosaville, Tewantin and the beach villages along the northern coast. It is a tightly planned area with its own council, its own planning rules and a market that draws buyers from all over the country.

What buying in Noosa is like

Noosa Shire has had its own council since 2014, separate from the rest of the Sunshine Coast, and its own planning scheme with strict controls on height, density and development. That is a large part of why the area looks the way it does, and why a buyer needs to check Noosa rules rather than assume Sunshine Coast ones.

The housing mix is wide: tourist and residential apartments around Hastings Street and Noosa Junction, riverfront homes and apartments along Gympie Terrace in Noosaville, canal homes at Noosa Waters, and family houses in Tewantin and the hill suburbs behind the beaches.

Who buys in Noosa

Retirees and downsizers, interstate and international buyers wanting a lifestyle base, holiday-home owners, families, and investors targeting holiday letting.

What to weigh up before you buy

Noosa is well loved, which makes it important to check the details:

  • Holiday letting rules. Many Noosa apartments sit in buildings set up for tourist accommodation, while others prohibit short stays. Zoning, by-laws and approvals decide what you can do. Confirm in writing.
  • River, canal and coastal hazards. Noosa River frontage and canal homes need flood and storm tide checks. Seawalls, pontoons and revetments need assessing too, and can be expensive to fix.
  • Building rules. Height and site-cover controls limit what you can build or extend, so a block's potential may be less than it looks.
  • Steep sites. Hillside streets deliver views but bring retaining walls, drainage and driveway issues.
  • Which Noosa. Buyers, rules and housing differ between Noosa Heads, Noosaville and Tewantin, so we start by working out which part suits your lifestyle.
