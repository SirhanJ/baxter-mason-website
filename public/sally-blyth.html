Meet the director & founder

Sally Blyth.

Director and founder of Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent, Sunshine Coast.

Director & founder

Sally Blyth, the director and founder of Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent Sunshine Coast, had a love for buying properties, property investing and developing that started well before she began working in the property industry and became a trusted buyers agent.

She personally bought her first house in 1996, which started her on a journey to financial freedom through property.

Tel: 0490 744 453
Email: sally@baxtermason.com.au

Empowering women through property

Dedicated to empowering other single, independent women to invest in their future through property ownership, Sally understands the challenges of buying property alone and is committed to helping others achieve their property dreams. Teamed with years of experience in the industry, Sally is equipped with the knowledge and passion to find the best property deals for her clients.

Sally understands how hard it is to buy property as a single woman, and how hard the decision-making process can be when there is only you to make the choice. She also understands property, all the steps involved from research, due diligence, negotiation and contracts, and she is here to help.

Local knowledge, tireless advocacy

She is a conscientious, knowledgeable and motivated local Sunshine Coast buyers agent who will work tirelessly to get you the best property that meets your criteria and budget. It is in her nature to help people, especially to help them find the perfect property. She enjoys the research, working out the strategy and negotiating hard for each and every client.

On a professional level, Sally is highly regarded amongst her peers for her reliability, ethical practices, honesty and professionalism.

I take the stress out of property buying by offering a comprehensive property buyers agent service. I take the time to get to know my clients and their needs and scope out and analyse the finest properties to suit their needs.

Sally Blyth
