Meet the director & founder

Sally Blyth.

Director and founder of Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent, Sunshine Coast.

Director & founder

Sally Blyth is the Director and Founder of Baxter and Mason, a respected Sunshine Coast buyers agency known for its personalised, data driven and client focused approach. With decades of experience buying, researching and investing in property, Sally brings a rare blend of lived experience, market knowledge and strategic insight to every purchase.

Sally bought her first property in 1996, beginning a lifelong commitment to building financial security through smart property decisions. She has purchased as a single woman, as a solo mum by choice raising two boys and as an investor building a long term portfolio. This personal journey gives her a deep understanding of the emotional and practical realities her clients face when buying a home or investment property.

Tel: 0490 744 453
Email: sally@baxtermason.com.au

A long standing connection to Buddina and the Sunshine Coast

After years of travelling and working overseas, Sally was drawn back to the Sunshine Coast for its beach lifestyle, community and sense of belonging. More than twenty five years ago she discovered Buddina and was fortunate to buy into the area early. Buddina has been home for Sally and her boys ever since, shaping her local knowledge and the way she supports clients who want to build a life on the Coast.

A strategic, data backed and empathetic approach

Sally combines empathy with strategy, using advanced technology and platforms like Vexur to analyse deals, assess risk, interpret feasibility reports and uncover off market opportunities. Her approach blends human understanding with data backed insights, giving clients clarity, confidence and a structured pathway through the buying process.

Local expertise and strong advocacy

Known for her reliability, professionalism and ethical approach, Sally works tirelessly to secure the right property for every client. She supports first home buyers, relocators, downsizers and investors across the Sunshine Coast, purchasing established homes, off market listings and investment properties from Caloundra to Noosa.

Empowering women through property

Sally is passionate about helping women create financial independence through property ownership. Many women feel overwhelmed by the buying process, especially those navigating separation, downsizing or buying alone. Sally provides guidance, advocacy and emotional support, helping women feel safe, informed and confident as they make major financial decisions.

A career built on purpose

For Sally, being a buyers agent is about more than real estate. It is about creating a life that reflects her values, setting an example for her boys and helping other families build long term security through property. Baxter and Mason is a woman led buyers agency built on heart, strategy and a genuine commitment to supporting clients through one of the most important decisions of their lives.

I take the stress out of property buying by offering a comprehensive property buyers agent service. I take the time to get to know my clients and their needs and scope out and analyse the finest properties to suit their needs.

Sally Blyth
