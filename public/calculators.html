Property tools

Property calculators.

Explore the numbers behind your next property decision.

Choose a calculator below to put numbers on a property decision before you commit to it. Each one is a guide only, but together they show what you can borrow, what a purchase will really cost and whether the figures stack up.

Before you start searching

Use the Borrowing Power calculator to estimate how much a lender might let you borrow, and the Income Tax calculator to see your take-home pay, which shapes what you can comfortably repay. If your deposit is still growing, the Savings and Savings Goal calculators show how regular contributions build and how long it takes to reach a target.

When you have found a property

The Stamp Duty calculator estimates the government cost of buying, which varies with price, property type and your circumstances. Concessions change, so confirm the current rules with the Queensland Revenue Office. The Buying Costs calculator adds other upfront costs, such as legal fees and inspections, so you know your real cash requirement. The Loan Repayment calculator shows repayments at different loan sizes, rates and terms.

Deciding whether to buy or invest

The Rent vs Buy calculator compares the long-term cost of renting with owning. The Rental Yield calculator shows the income a property could earn relative to its price, which helps compare investment options, although yield is only one part of the picture.

These tools give general estimates, not financial or tax advice, so your broker, accountant and lender should confirm the final figures. If you would like help turning the results into a brief, book a discovery call.
