Got questions? We've got answers.

Practical answers about how our Sunshine Coast buyers agency works, the suburbs we cover from Caloundra to Noosa, our fees, and what makes Baxter & Mason different.

The answer is a resounding yes. On the Sunshine Coast, where competition for good stock is fierce and a large share of quality property never reaches realestate.com.au, a buyers agent usually pays for themselves. We open doors to off-market and pre-market listings, we negotiate on your behalf rather than the vendor's, and we stop you overpaying in a heated market or buying a property with problems you cannot see.

Our fees are transparent and agreed up front, so you know exactly what you are investing before we begin. For most clients the saving on purchase price, plus the weekends of searching and the emotional toll they get back, more than covers the cost.

We are a boutique Sunshine Coast buyers agency offering full search and acquisition, negotiation-only and auction bidding services. We work with first home buyers, families upgrading or downsizing, interstate relocators, and investors buying both established homes and new builds.

A full service covers your brief and budget, suburb and street-level research from Caloundra to Noosa, shortlisting and inspecting properties in person, appraisal and due diligence, negotiation or auction representation, and support right through to settlement. We also offer property coaching for buyers who want to build the skills to buy well themselves, and dedicated support for women through Her Key To Confidence.

Finding the right property can feel daunting, especially if you are buying on the Sunshine Coast from interstate or overseas. We start with a detailed brief covering your budget, timeline, must-haves and deal breakers, then translate that into the specific suburbs and streets that actually suit how you want to live.

From there we use our network of local selling agents, developers and industry contacts to surface on-market, pre-market and off-market opportunities, and we physically inspect them so you are only looking at properties worth your time. Sally's 30 years in design and four years in defect inspection mean we also flag build quality, flood and orientation issues that photos never show.

We are property advisors, and we recommend properties based on your brief, your budget and what the local Sunshine Coast market is genuinely doing. We will tell you when a property is the wrong buy, even when that is not what you want to hear.

For investment structures, borrowing capacity, tax and depreciation advice, we work alongside your financial planner, accountant and mortgage broker rather than replacing them. If you do not have those people yet, we can introduce you to trusted local professionals we work with regularly.

Yes. Our fees cover the full engagement: property research and suburb analysis, inspections and appraisals, due diligence, negotiation or auction bidding, and guidance through to settlement. They are quoted and agreed in writing before we start, so there are no surprises.

We only ever act for buyers. We do not accept commissions, kickbacks or referral fees from selling agents or developers, which means our advice is not influenced by anyone but you.

The team has bought through boom, correction and recovery on the Sunshine Coast, so the advice you get is grounded in how this market actually behaves rather than national headlines. You can read more about Sally and Kelly and see what that looks like in practice on our success stories page.

We stand out for our boutique approach and personalised service. Unlike selling agents, who work for the vendor, we exclusively represent buyers, so your interests are the only ones on the table. We deliberately take on a small number of clients at a time so you deal with Sally directly rather than being handed to a junior.

Our edge is the technical eye. Three decades in design and four years inspecting defects means we read a property for build quality, orientation, flood risk and renovation potential, not just presentation. We know the Sunshine Coast street by street from Caloundra to Noosa and into the hinterland, and we are passionate about empowering buyers, particularly women, to secure their financial future through property.

Negotiation is where a buyers agent earns their keep. We establish what a Sunshine Coast property is genuinely worth using recent comparable sales rather than the asking price, then build a strategy around the vendor's actual motivation, timeline and preferred terms.

Because we deal with local selling agents every week, they take our offers seriously and often tell us things a private buyer would never hear. We also represent clients at auction, which removes the emotion that causes people to bid past their limit, and we structure conditions, deposits and settlement dates to make your offer the most attractive one on the table without simply paying more.

Due diligence covers building and pest, flood and bushfire mapping, body corporate records, easements and zoning, plus checks on rental potential where relevant. With four years of defect inspection experience, Sally routinely picks up issues in both established homes and brand new builds that buyers would otherwise inherit after settlement.

We monitor the industry databases and sales data daily, but the real advantage is relationships. We speak with Sunshine Coast selling agents, developers, brokers and property managers constantly, which is how we hear about pre-market and off-market opportunities before they are advertised.

A significant share of the properties our clients buy were never publicly listed. If you have been watching the portals and feeling like the good homes disappear before you can get to them, that is usually why.

We cover the full Sunshine Coast, from Caloundra in the south to Noosa in the north, and west into the hinterland. Whether you want beachfront with ocean views, a family home near good schools, or a quiet hinterland retreat on acreage, we know the trade-offs street by street.

Suburbs we buy in regularly include Caloundra, Kings Beach, Moffat Beach, Dicky Beach, Golden Beach, Pelican Waters, Currimundi, Wurtulla, Buddina, Minyama, Mooloolaba, Alexandra Headland, Maroochydore, Kuluin, Buderim, Mountain Creek, Sippy Downs, Twin Waters, Marcoola, Mudjimba, Coolum Beach, Peregian Beach, Sunshine Beach, Noosa, Bli Bli, Nambour, Woombye, Palmwoods, Montville and Maleny. If your suburb is not listed, ask us anyway.

Most of our Sunshine Coast clients are under contract within one to three months of engaging us, which covers the search, inspections, negotiation and due diligence. Standard Queensland contracts then usually settle 30 days later.

Timeframes move with the brief. A well-funded buyer with a flexible brief can be under contract in a fortnight, and we have secured properties for clients in under a week. A tight budget, an unusual property type, or a narrow pocket with very little turnover will take longer, and we would rather take an extra month than push you into the wrong purchase.

A selling agent works for the vendor. A buyer's agent works for you. At Baxter & Mason, that means honest advice, strong negotiation, and no mixed loyalties.
