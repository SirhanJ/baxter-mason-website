Commercial property

Commercial property has long been the quiet achiever of Australian investing. While residential markets rise and fall with sentiment, commercial assets offer predictable income, stronger yields, long leases, and quality tenants, making them one of the most reliable ways to build long-term wealth.

For investors across the Sunshine Coast, commercial property provides a powerful combination of stability, performance, and opportunity. Whether you're building a portfolio, diversifying your strategy, or stepping into commercial for the first time, Baxter & Mason is here to guide you through every step.

A smarter, stronger investment pathway

Commercial property is a specialist space. Lease structures, outgoings, tenant strength, zoning, incentives, yields, and market positioning all play a critical role in performance. We are in the Sunshine Coast commercial market daily, reviewing deals, analysing leases, speaking with agents, and securing opportunities before they reach the public market.

Why commercial property performs

  • Higher net yields, typically 5.5%-6.5%, offering stronger cashflow
  • Longer leases, often 3-10 years, providing predictable, stable income
  • Quality tenants, established businesses with lower turnover
  • Lower volatility, markets that move more steadily
  • Accessible entry points from around $500,000
  • Clearer value drivers: income, lease strength, tenant quality, and location

Commercial property is powerful, but more complex

Unlike residential, commercial property requires deeper understanding and sharper analysis. This is where most investors feel overwhelmed, and where Baxter & Mason steps in. Commercial is not a DIY investment. It is your biggest asset, and it deserves expert eyes, expert strategy, and expert execution.

Who commercial property suits

  • Investors seeking strong, stable income
  • SMSFs wanting long-term, income-producing assets
  • Business owners wanting to own their premises
  • Portfolio builders wanting diversification
  • Anyone seeking lower volatility and predictable returns
