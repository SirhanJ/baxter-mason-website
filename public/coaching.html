Property coaching

Property investing can feel overwhelming, especially when you are trying to make smart decisions that support your long-term financial goals. After personally buying, building, renovating and holding multiple investment properties, and spending the first four years of her buyers agent career working exclusively with investors, Sally learned one thing clearly:

The right property changes everything. The wrong one costs you time, money, and momentum.

Our Property Coaching service is designed to give you clarity, confidence, and a proven framework for choosing investment properties that genuinely support your financial direction, without the guesswork, overwhelm, or costly mistakes.

Why work with a property coach?

Most investors do not fail because they lack motivation. They fail because they lack strategy. Sally helps you understand what type of property best suits your financial goals, which suburbs and asset types perform differently, how to assess growth, yield and long-term performance, how to avoid common investor mistakes, and how to build a portfolio that grows safely and sustainably.

This is not generic advice. It is grounded in real experience, real deals, and real outcomes.

My experience, your advantage

This is not theory. It is what Sally has lived. She has bought, built, renovated and held multiple investment properties, including dual-income builds, high-yield assets and long-term capital growth plays. For the first four years of working in real estate, she worked solely with investors across the Sunshine Coast and beyond, helping them select the right investment property, understand market cycles, identify high-performing pockets, and build portfolios aligned with their financial goals.

What you'll learn

  • Strategy & goal setting
  • Market education
  • Property selection framework
  • Portfolio building
  • Real-world examples from actual deals
  • Negotiation, due diligence & offer strategy

What makes this different?

There are plenty of property experts online. Very few have lived the investment journey personally, worked exclusively with investors for years, hold deep Sunshine Coast market knowledge, and maintain real relationships with agents, builders and developers, with a proven track record of successful investor outcomes. This is coaching backed by experience, not theory.

Who this coaching is for

  • First-time investors
  • Existing investors wanting to level up
  • Homeowners transitioning into investment
  • Anyone wanting clarity before buying
  • People who want to avoid expensive mistakes

Important: Sally is not a financial planner and does not provide personalised financial advice. All guidance offered through Property Coaching is general in nature. You should seek independent financial advice to ensure any investment strategy suits your personal circumstances.
