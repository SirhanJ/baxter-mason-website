A Dream Holiday Home Secured Off-Market

June 22, 2026 · Success Story · 2 min read

When this wonderful family reached out to Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent, they had a clear vision: a fabulous holiday home they could escape to year after year, one that also doubled as a smart long-term investment on the Sunshine Coast.

They wanted something close to the beach, close to lifestyle, and close to everything that makes the Sunshine Coast such a special place to unwind. But they also wanted to avoid the stress, competition, and inflated prices that come with buying in a high-demand coastal market.

The search for the perfect coastal investment

From the outset, Sally focused on properties that offered both lifestyle and capital growth potential. The brief was clear:

  • Walking distance to Alexandra Headland's iconic beaches
  • Close to the Maroochydore CBD and emerging city centre
  • Strong long-term rental and holiday-letting appeal
  • A home that felt warm, welcoming, and perfect for family memories

With her deep local connections and strong agent relationships, Sally uncovered an off-market opportunity that ticked every box, before it ever reached the public.

Secured off-market, no competition, no stress

This property was a rare find: a beautifully positioned home in one of the Sunshine Coast's most desirable lifestyle pockets, secured without competition, without bidding pressure, and without the emotional rollercoaster that often comes with coastal buying.

Sally's negotiation strategy, market insight, and calm, patient approach ensured the family secured the property at the right price and with complete confidence.

A holiday home to treasure, and a smart investment

Today, the family are the proud owners of a fabulous holiday home that will be enjoyed for generations. It's already become their favourite getaway, a place to relax, reconnect, and create lifelong memories.

But it is also a sound long-term investment, well placed between the beach and Maroochydore.

"Her patience and dedication were tireless."

Sally searched hard, guided the family through every decision, and ensured the property aligned with both their lifestyle and financial goals.

They now have the holiday home they wanted, and a straightforward path to get there.

Sally Blyth

Sally is the founder and director of Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent. She bought her first property in 1996 and has been helping buyers, especially women building independence on their own, ever since.
