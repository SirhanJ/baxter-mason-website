Success Story

Buyer Win for Hospital Workers

May 5, 2026 · Success Story · 1 min read

A young couple both work in Birtinya and wanted to minimise commute time. As first home buyers they had a firm budget of $1,000,000 and a clear brief: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, double lock-up garage, modern and move-in ready, as close to Birtinya as possible.

The Challenge

Affordable, modern homes close to Birtinya were limited and out of budget. Many options in nearby suburbs would have created long peak-hour commutes, which the clients wanted to avoid. The couple needed a property that balanced short commute, turnkey condition and strict budget constraints, and they wanted the process handled with minimal stress.

Our Approach

Clarified priorities so commute time and turnkey condition remained non-negotiable while allowing flexibility on exact pocket.

Targeted pre-market and off-market opportunities in Birtinya and immediate surrounds to find properties before wider competition.

Negotiated proactively and managed inspections, contract terms and communications with the selling agent to protect the clients through the purchase.

Kept clients informed at every step so Synn and Jeff could make confident decisions without the usual stress of house hunting.

The Outcome

Sally secured a roomy, park-facing terraced house in Birtinya that matched the brief: 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, double lock-up garage, found pre-market and purchased within the $1,000,000 budget. The home is modern, move-in ready and a short commute to work, exactly what Synn and Jeff wanted. The purchase was completed smoothly thanks to continuous communication and hands-on management from search through settlement.

Why This Worked

Clear brief and priorities enabled a focused search. Local market knowledge identified a pre-auction opportunity close to Birtinya. Persistent negotiation and relationship management with agents secured the property before wider competition. End-to-end handling removed stress and saved the clients time.

Sally Blyth

Sally is the founder and director of Baxter & Mason Property Buyers Agent. She bought her first property in 1996 and has been helping buyers, especially women building independence on their own, ever since.
